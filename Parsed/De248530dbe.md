Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF) is alarmed by the dramatic escalation in conflict across the Middle East region, which began on 28 February following strikes by US and Israeli forces in Iran and Iran’s subsequent retaliatory actions in several countries. While ceasefires have brought needed respite for some, our teams in Iran and Lebanon are seeing that access to essential medical care remains fragile in both countries.

Across the region, the escalation in violence has brought fear to the lives of millions of people, including in Lebanon, Iran, and the Gulf countries. Thousands of people have been killed or injured across the region, and millions have been forced from their homes, often multiple times and with little or no possessions. In Lebanon, violence continues despite the temporary ceasefire.

MSF calls for the protection of civilians, hospitals, health facilities, and other essential infrastructure.

Our teams are adapting our programmes and/or preparing to respond in different countries, and we are closely monitoring the rapidly evolving humanitarian needs.
