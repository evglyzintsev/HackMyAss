CHRONOLOGY 
OF EVENTS 1995-1998
The main purpose of this chronology is to help the reader by reconstructing MSF’s 
actions and public statements in regional and international news reports of the 
period. It is intended as a tool for this specific document, and not as an academic 
reference.

1910
Japan formalises its occupation of Korea.
1945
Japan’s defeat – Korean peninsula split in two by the 
38th parallel. Soviet administration and troops to the 
North and the US to the South.
1948
Two separate Korean governments are established: the 
pro-Soviet North (Kim Il Sung as Prime Minister) and 
the pro-US South.
1950
June 1950
Southern invasion by northern troops claiming to 
reunify Korea.
Korean war: North backed by USSR and China, South 
backed by UN forces (mostly US).
1953
JULY 1953
27 July 1953
Ceasefire in Panmunjom: no peace treaty.
1972
Kim Il Sung President of the Democratic People’s 
Republic of Korea (and Secretary General of the Labor 
Party).
